A journal you talk to.

Speaking is faster than writing and truer than typing — you say the thing you actually meant before you have time to tidy it. speakjoy listens, writes it down in your hand, and leaves it on the page where it belongs.

How it goes

Press once and speak

No dictation button to hold down. It hears when you stop and closes the take itself.

It writes itself down

Transcribed, punctuated and broken into paragraphs, then set on a ruled page in a handwriting face.

It stays a journal

Dated, bound into a book you chose the cloth for, and searchable when you want to find it again.

The details

Made like a book, not a text box.

Everything here exists because a real journal has it: paper you chose, ink that stays the same, a date at the head of the day, room for a photograph.

Your own hand

Six handwriting faces, set on a baseline grid so the words sit on the rule rather than float above it.

Cloth and ink

Thirty-nine bindings from pale to oxblood to black, and five inks — chosen per journal.

One day, one head

The date is written once. Speak again the same day and it carries only the hour.

Photographs

Print, taped, corners, deckle or bare. Sized to the page so a picture never becomes the entry.

Guided prompts

Collections by part of life — gratitude, stress and anxiety, work and success — kept and carried into a session.

Find anything

Search everything you have ever said. Every entry, every journal, by keyword.
